# Referência de volume e movimento

Modo: ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, geração de uma prancha original.
Uso: estudo de formas e poses; não integra os PNGs finais do jogo.
O texto abaixo é o prompt enviado, preservado em seu idioma original.

Mature elongated proportions with a SMALL helmet/head, approximately five heads tall, not cute, not toy-like. Show for EACH character one large expertly shaded idle sprite and a sequence of SIX clearly distinct full-body action poses, all facing right: knight sword attack with low anticipation, backward wind-up, forward hip-and-shoulder rotation, extended cut with sharp crescent smear, low follow-through and recovery; mage spell cast with compressed crouch, rising charge, lifted staff, full-body forward release, recoil and recovery. Dramatic but readable mass shifts, planted feet, bent knees, overlapped cape motion. Pixel craftsmanship: compact coherent clusters, strong light from upper left, shadow hue shifting, selective outlines, metallic specular highlight clusters, rich fabric fold volumes, low noise. Designed to be reconstructed at 32x32 pixels per frame, allow a few larger isolated studies for readable analysis. Consistent original character identity across poses. Dark neutral blue background, organized rows with generous spacing, no labels, no text, no borrowed game characters or copied sprites.
