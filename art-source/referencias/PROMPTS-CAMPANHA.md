# Referências geradas para a campanha

Estas imagens foram geradas com `image_gen` e importadas no Aseprite em display virtual. Os exports de jogo usam recorte, redução nearest, transparência e a paleta indexada compartilhada do projeto.

## Cenários

> Create a finished pixel-art environment atlas for a serious dark fantasy side-scroller game, matching a 480x270 moody hand-pixeled background aesthetic. One single image split into exactly two equal vertical panels with no divider and no text. LEFT: an abandoned underground iron mine, ancient stone supports, rusted rails, iron ore veins, cold blue-black shadows, restrained amber lantern light, layered parallax depth, clear empty gameplay floor area across the lower third. RIGHT: ruined arcane observatory at night, shattered gothic arches, enormous broken astrolabe, distant storm sky and stars, dark indigo stone, restrained violet and gold runes, layered depth, clear empty gameplay floor across the lower third. Serious fantasy, weathered materials, crisp deliberate pixel clusters, high detail, no characters, no UI, no logos, no writing, no blur, no gradients pretending to be pixel art. Landscape 16:9 composition.

## Sentinela e Guardião

Cada folha pede seis linhas de oito quadros: `idle`, `windup`, `attack`, `recovery`, `hurt` e `death`. A Sentinela usa ferro negro, bronze e uma picareta integrada; o Guardião usa pedra, bronze, uma auréola de astrolábio e uma arma crescente. Os prompts exigem antecipação, peso, continuidade, baseline constante, efeitos contidos e ausência de pixels estranhos ou partes cortadas.

## Recursos, passagens e construções

> Create a clean production pixel-art prop atlas for a serious dark fantasy side-scroller, transparent background, no text, no grid lines, no borders. Arrange exactly 8 distinct isolated props in one horizontal row, evenly spaced and centered on the same baseline, each fully contained: 1 jagged iron ore deposit with cold steel glints, 2 faceted arcane crystal cluster with restrained violet-cyan inner glow, 3 sealed mine gate of black iron and chains, 4 sealed observatory gate with broken brass astrolabe lock, 5 rebuilt field shelter of dark timber and burgundy cloth, 6 compact forge workshop with anvil and dim ember, 7 weapon arsenal rack under a reinforced awning, 8 restored miniature observatory with brass rings and violet lens. Serious weathered medieval fantasy, readable side-view silhouettes, crisp deliberate hand-pixeled clusters, detailed materials and shadows, consistent scale, no cute style, no characters, no scenery, no labels, no stray pixels. Wide sprite-sheet composition.
